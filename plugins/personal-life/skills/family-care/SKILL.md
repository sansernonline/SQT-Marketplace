## Check before done

- Never diagnose, never say "it is probably normal", and never suggest a medicine, supplement or dose. The doctor, midwife or vet decides.
- Danger signs in pregnancy (bleeding, water breaking, seizure, severe headache, fainting) → **call 1669 or go to the hospital now**, before anything else in the answer.
- The ID number, blood group and health details stay in a private file. Never post them in a group chat.
- A shareable table shows "ยา ตามตาราง" and a time, not drug names.
- Say once that this is general information, not medical, veterinary, tax or legal advice.
- A whole care setup with several steps → playbook `superuser/references/playbook-care-setup.md`.
